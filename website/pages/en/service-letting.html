Letting your home

Letting a home starts with finding the right tenant. Pandt Property Management takes care of letting your property: from presenting it and arranging viewings to a clear rental agreement that is signed online.

What Pandt takes care of.

  • Viewings with prospective tenants

  • Handover to day-to-day management

Who it is for

Is this for you?

For owners who want to let a home, or need a new tenant, without organising it themselves. Whether it is one property or several, you have one point of contact from the first viewing to the signed agreement.

How it works

Arranged in 4 steps.

  1. Introduction

    You tell us about the property and what you want from letting it.

  2. Presentation and viewings

    We present your home and arrange viewings with prospective tenants.

  3. Rental agreement

    With the new tenant we put everything in a clear rental agreement, signed online.

  4. Management

    If you wish, the home then moves on to our day-to-day management.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about letting.

Can I outsource only the letting?

We discuss which tasks you want to outsource during the introduction. You then receive a clear proposal setting out what Pandt will take care of for you.

How is the rental agreement signed?

The rental agreement is signed online, so everyone has a signed copy straight away and everything is in writing.

What happens after the home is let?

If you also leave the management to us, the home moves on to day-to-day management once the agreement is signed: monthly invoices, tenant contact and maintenance.
