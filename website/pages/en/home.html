About Pandt

Renting should be simple.

Pandt Property Management lets and manages homes in the Netherlands. As a tenant you have one dedicated point of contact with us: for your tenancy agreement, your monthly invoice, repairs and all your questions.

We are easy to reach, put agreements in writing and deal with repairs quickly.

How it works

New with Pandt? Sorted in 4 steps.

  1. Send your details

    You fill in the form: your contact details, a co-tenant if you rent together, and the address of your home.

  2. We check

    We check your details and set everything up in our administration.

  3. Email from us

    You receive an email as soon as everything is ready.

  4. All set

    You receive a clear invoice every month and know where to go with questions.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions.

How do I send my details?

On the page Send your details. You fill in your contact details and the address of your home. It takes about 2 minutes. You receive an email as soon as everything is ready.

How and when do I pay the rent?

Every month you receive a clear invoice from us with the amount and the due date.

Can I change my details later?

Yes. Email your new details to beheer@pandtbeheer.nl and we will update them.
