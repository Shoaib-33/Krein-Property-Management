Who it is for

Is this for you?

For private landlords, investors with a small portfolio and owners letting their home temporarily. You keep control of your property; we take the day-to-day work off your hands.

How it works

Arranged in 4 steps.

  1. Introduction

    We discuss your property, its current situation and what you would like to outsource.

  2. Proposal and agreements

    You receive a clear proposal. All agreements are put in writing.

  3. Handover

    We take over the administration and tenant contact, and let your tenant know.

  4. Day-to-day management

    Monthly invoices, payments, questions and repairs: we keep it all in order, with one dedicated point of contact for you.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about property management.

What does a property manager do?

A property manager looks after the day-to-day running of a rental home on the owner’s behalf: letting, rent administration, contact with the tenant and coordinating maintenance and repairs. You decide which tasks you outsource.

What does property management with Pandt cost?

The cost depends on the property and the services you choose. After an introduction you receive a clear proposal setting out all costs.

How do I keep track of my property?

You have one dedicated point of contact and are kept informed about important matters, such as a new tenant or a repair. Everything agreed is in writing, so you always know what has been arranged.
