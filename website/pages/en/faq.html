Frequently asked questions.

Answers for landlords and tenants. Can’t find your question? Email beheer@pandtbeheer.nl.

What does a property manager do?

A property manager looks after the day-to-day running of a rental home on the owner’s behalf: letting, rent administration, contact with the tenant and coordinating maintenance and repairs. You decide which tasks you outsource. What we agree is set out in a management agreement.

What does property management with Pandt cost?

The cost depends on the property and the services you choose. After an introduction you receive a clear proposal setting out all costs.

Which areas does Pandt cover?

Pandt manages homes in the Netherlands. Enter the address or postcode of your property in your enquiry and we will quickly let you know whether we can manage it.

How do I keep track of my property while Pandt manages it?

You have one dedicated point of contact and are kept informed about important matters, such as a new tenant or a repair. Everything agreed is in writing, so you always know what has been arranged.

How does Pandt handle maintenance and repairs?

Tenants report maintenance to us. We assess the report, bring in a tradesperson and keep you informed until the repair is done.

Does Pandt follow Dutch rental law?

Yes. We work in line with current Dutch rental law, including the rules on rental agreements and good landlordship.

How do I send my details?

Through the Send your details page. You fill in your contact details and the address of your home. It takes about 2 minutes, and you receive an email once everything is set up.

Which details do you ask for?

Your name, email address, phone number, preferred language and the address of the home, and those of a co-tenant if you rent together. We never ask for your PIN, login codes, a copy of your ID or your BSN.

How do I report a repair or a fault?

Email beheer@pandtbeheer.nl with a short description and, if possible, a photo. We will arrange a tradesperson. In case of fire, a gas smell or any dangerous situation, always call 112 first.

How and when do I pay the rent?

You receive a clear invoice from us every month, showing the amount and the due date.

Can I change my details later?

Yes. Email your new details to beheer@pandtbeheer.nl and we will update them.
