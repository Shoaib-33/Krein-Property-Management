Who it is for

Is this for you?

For owners who would rather not be called about a dripping tap or a broken boiler, but do want to know it is being dealt with properly.

How it works

Arranged in 4 steps.

  1. Report

    Your tenant reports the problem to us, with a description and, if possible, a photo.

  2. Assessment

    We assess what needs to be done.

  3. Tradesperson

    We bring in a tradesperson and schedule the repair with the tenant.

  4. Completion

    We follow the repair up until it is done and keep you informed.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about maintenance and technical management.

How does a tenant report a repair?

Your tenant reports the problem to us with a short description and, if possible, a photo. In case of fire, a gas smell or any dangerous situation, the tenant always calls 112 first.

Will I as the owner be kept informed?

Yes. We keep both owner and tenant informed about what is happening until the repair is done.

Who carries out the repairs?

We bring in a tradesperson suited to the problem and follow the repair up until it is done.
