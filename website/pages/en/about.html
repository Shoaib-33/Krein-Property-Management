Who we are

Personal, reliable and close by.

Pandt Property Management lets and manages residential property in the Netherlands on behalf of owners. We take care of everything around letting: from the rental agreement and monthly invoice to repairs and maintenance. Owners can count on careful management; tenants have one clear point of contact.

We believe letting and renting should be simple. That is why we are easy to reach, put agreements in writing and deal with repairs quickly.

How we work with owners

You stay in control, we do the work.

Every partnership starts with an introduction. We talk through your property and which tasks you would like to outsource, and put what we agree in writing. You then have one dedicated point of contact, who keeps you informed about important matters such as a new tenant or a repair.

  • One point of contact
  • Agreements in writing
  • Quick answers
  • Careful with your details and your tenants’ details
