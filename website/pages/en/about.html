Who we are

Personal, reliable and close by.

Pandt Property Management lets and manages homes in the Netherlands. We take care of everything around the tenancy: from the tenancy agreement and monthly invoice to repairs and maintenance. As a tenant you have one clear point of contact with us.

We believe renting should be simple. That is why we are easy to reach, put agreements in writing and deal with repairs quickly.
