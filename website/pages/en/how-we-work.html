How we work.

Working with Pandt Property Management starts with a conversation and ends with a home that is properly looked after every day. This is how we go about it.

  1. Introduction

    You tell us about your home and what you expect from management. We explain how we work, so you know what to expect.

    What you get from us: a clear picture of what Pandt can take care of for your property.

  2. Discussing the home and your wishes

    We go through the property, its current situation (vacant or let) and which tasks you would like to outsource: only the letting, the day-to-day management, or both.

    What you get from us: an overview of the tasks we have agreed together.

  3. Proposal and agreements

    You receive a clear proposal setting out all costs. Everything we agree is put in writing, so you always know what has been arranged.

    What you get from us: a clear proposal and written agreements.

  4. Getting started

    We take over the management and inform the tenant. From then on you have one point of contact, and so does your tenant.

    What you get from us: one dedicated point of contact for everything to do with your home.
