How we work together

From introduction to management in four steps.

  1. Introduction

    You tell us about your home and what you expect from management. We explain how we work.

  2. Discussing the home and your wishes

    We go through the property, its current situation and which tasks you would like to outsource.

  3. Proposal and agreements

    You receive a clear proposal. Everything we agree is put in writing.

  4. Getting started

    We take over the management and inform the tenant. From then on you have one point of contact.
